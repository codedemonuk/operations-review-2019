## Performance

+++

### Performance Management Arrangements in 2018/19

Note:

- 2020 Vision, Corporate Plan for Hertsmere published for the period 2017 - 2020

- Alongside the overarching plan:
  - A retrospective Annual Report which details the Council’s achievements against each priority area over the last year
  - A forward looking annual Action Plan which sets out the key areas of work under each Corporate priority for the year ahead.
- To support this, a new Performance Management Framework was introduced.
  In line with the plan it significantly reduced the number of PIs being reported corporately to ensure an increased focus on key services.
  15 Quality Service Indicators (QSIs) have been selected to reflect the core council services and are closely monitored to ensure high levels of service delivery.
  A wider set of Key Performance Indicators are reviewed by the Executive Performance Panel on an exception basis and Senior Officers maintain an overview across all 100 corporately collected PIs.

- Performance Management now takes place as follows:
  - Corporate Action Plan and Quality Service Indicators – quarterly updates to Chief Officers, Executive Performance Management Panel and Executive.
  - Key Performance Indicators – quarterly update to Chief Officers and by exception to Executive Performance Management Panel.
  - Management Information Indicators – quarterly update to Chief Officers
- Issues requiring further investigation are referred to the relevant Scrutiny Committee.

+++

### Deelopments for 2019/20

Note:

Following on from an Audit of Data Quality which took place during 2018, an updated Data Quality Policy Statement will be introduced for 2019/20.  This document articulates the Council’s commitment to data quality with a focus on the Quality Service Indicator set.  All QSIs will require a supporting Audit Trail to be completed which identifies the source of the data along with checks made to ensure the accuracy of the data being reported.

## IT

+++

### Business Support and Projects Team

Note:

- The team have managed major system upgrades of the Planning, Land Charges, Income Management and Finance systems. They have led on successful new system implementations of the Environmental Health, Licensing and PCI Pal (PCI compliance) systems.
- Phase one of moving the payment system to a fully hosted platform was completed. The final phases of this project will be completed in Q2/3 2019.
- The Business and Projects Team have developed good working relationships with all areas of the Council and are integral to the organisation providing a high level of customer service to our residents and businesses.
- The team have worked closely with colleagues in Planning to implement mobile apps on iPads for Planning officers. Work has started on implementing mobile apps on iPads for Enforcement officers. This work has supported to Planning in their move towards an agile workforce.
- The team are currently preparing for Council Tax billing, Housing Benefits and Financial year end processing. The team prides itself on maintaining minimal system downtime at year-end and when system upgrades are required throughout the year by working evenings and at weekends.

+++

### Infrastructure Team

Note:

- During the year the Infrastructure Team have migrated all systems running on older servers and storage units to a supplier-supported environment. Equipment and operating systems no longer supported by the supplier have been decommissioned. This is required to maintain our central government Public Services Network (PSN) compliance.
- The corporate Wi-Fi system was replaced in the summer giving an improved service to staff, Members and the public visiting the Civic offices.
- The team successfully completed a major project to replace Members iPads and to migrate Members and staff who used the Good app to Microsoft Office 365 cloud platform. The internal email server was also upgraded which will allow other staff to be migrated to MS365 in the future.
- As with the Business Support Team, the Infrastructure Team prides itself in keeping system downtime to a minimum by working evenings and weekends when applying essential system updates.

+++

### Service Desk Team

Note:

- The service desk team have supported the project to replace Members iPads and the move to MS365. The initial 2-year contract to support Hertfordshire Building Control ended in August but IDS are continuing support on a rolling monthly basis. The service desk team have continued to provide a high level of first level support to the organisation.
- A minor restructure of the team allowed the permanent appointment of a Service Desk Administrator and the recruitment of a second IDS Apprentice. The cost of the apprenticeship training is being funded from the apprenticeship levy. Both apprentices are Hertsmere residents and are a great asset to the team.

+++

### Technical Security

Note:

The threat of a cyber-attack is increasing with a number of high profile successful attacks in the media in the last year. The team have worked hard with limited resources to protect the Councils assets and reputation. Our cyber defences are continually reviewed and updated. The team monitor the Councils systems overnight and at weekends.
In a typical month:
The email filter blocks 130 Viruses.
28,500 Spams emails are rejected.
10,500 emails are quarantined for review by the infrastructure team.
475 cyber intrusion events occurred.

+++

### Ongoing Projects

Note:

- Information and Digital Services are working with the Society of IT Managers (SOCITM) on a project to look at the feasibility of migrating current systems to a public cloud. This is a major project and one few councils have undertaken. A migration to the cloud could reduce costs, support agile working and free up property assets.
- Work has begun on replacing the desktop infrastructure. The current desktop PCs and laptops are now 6 years old. A refresh will give an opportunity for the organisation to look at new and different ways of working. IDS are working with Chief Officers, the Head of HR and Customer Services and Service Heads on finding the best solution for Hertsmere.
- Hertsmere have partnered with Oxford City Council and 11 other local authorities in a successful Local Digital funding bid for a discovery project to look at the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and chatbots in local government. The four areas the project will research are Planning, Housing Benefits, Highways, Waste, and recycling. This exciting project could lead to further partnership working in the future.
- IDS have worked closely with the Payroll Manager in securing the contract to provide payroll services to Hertfordshire Building Control.
- As you can see, it has been a varied and busy year for IDS with all members of the team contributing and playing a full part in providing a professional IT support service to the organisation.

## Human Resources

Note:

In 2018 we achieved the following:

+++

### Health and Wellbeing

Note:

- For most months of the year we met our cumulative target for short term sickness absence of no more than 3.5 days per FTE. It is currently fractionally above the target at 3.53 days per FTE which is to be expected in the winter season.
- We delivered successful health and well-being events and have expanded our range of initiatives on offer

+++

### Organisation Changes

Note:

- We successfully implemented organisational change in a number of departments
- We increased the number of apprenticeship opportunities to 7
- We resolved a number of complex employee relations cases
- We recruited to a number of hard to fill posts including the appointment of a new Head of Planning and Economic Development who commenced on 7 January 2019
- We improved the ratio of young people (16-24 year olds) in the workforce

+++

### Policies

Note:

- We implemented a comprehensive Health and Safety work programme in partnership with HCC and reviewed key policies such as the Abusive Behaviour Policy
- We have carried out comprehensive staff consultation on a new Workforce Strategy for 2019-2021 (more than 120 employees were directly involved in consultation meetings)
- We have reviewed and implemented key policies including the Recruitment and Retention Policy, modernising the process including on-line microsites and use of social media, the most recent example being the Head of Planning

+++

### Other work

Note:

- Prepared for the implementation of an upgraded HR/Payroll system and employee kiosk
- We have worked closely with the LJC on the development of new staff facilities including a new staff room which opens onto the roof garden
- We have organised training and consultancy to support the organisation in relation to GDPR and implemented measures to comply with GDPR in HR
- We achieved a good outcome from the Gender Pay Gap Analysis and published this in accordance with the legislative timetable as well as reporting it as part of the annual pay statement to full council

- The Workforce plan will be coming to the Personnel Committee in February and will set out the action plan for HR work for the year.
