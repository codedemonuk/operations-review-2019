## Human Resources

Note:

In 2018 we achieved the following:

+++

### Health and Wellbeing

Note:

* For most months of the year we met our cumulative target for short term sickness absence of no more than 3.5 days per FTE. It is currently fractionally above the target at 3.53 days per FTE which is to be expected in the winter season.
* We delivered successful health and well-being events and have expanded our range of initiatives on offer

+++

### Organisation Changes

Note:

* We successfully implemented organisational change in a number of departments
* We increased the number of apprenticeship opportunities to 7
* We resolved a number of complex employee relations cases
* We recruited to a number of hard to fill posts including the appointment of a new Head of Planning and Economic Development who commenced on 7 January 2019
* We improved the ratio of young people (16-24 year olds) in the workforce

+++

### Policies

Note:

* We implemented a comprehensive Health and Safety work programme in partnership with HCC and reviewed key policies such as the Abusive Behaviour Policy
* We have carried out comprehensive staff consultation on a new Workforce Strategy for 2019-2021 (more than 120 employees were directly involved in consultation meetings)
* We have reviewed and implemented key policies including the Recruitment and Retention Policy, modernising the process including on-line microsites and use of social media, the most recent example being the Head of Planning

+++

### Other work

Note:

* Prepared for the implementation of an upgraded HR/Payroll system and employee kiosk
* We have worked closely with the LJC on the development of new staff facilities including a new staff room which opens onto the roof garden
* We have organised training and consultancy to support the organisation in relation to GDPR and implemented measures to comply with GDPR in HR
* We achieved a good outcome from the Gender Pay Gap Analysis and published this in accordance with the legislative timetable as well as reporting it as part of the annual pay statement to full council

* The Workforce plan will be coming to the Personnel Committee in February and will set out the action plan for HR work for the year.
